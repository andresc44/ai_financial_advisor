# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Finnbot is a personal project run by its owner, Andres, for the owner's own use. It is not a public service.

## What the app accesses

Finnbot connects to the owner's own Google Drive account through Google's OAuth. It uses that access for one purpose: to copy backups of Finnbot's own data and log files into folders in the owner's Drive.

## What it does with your data

- The app only reads and writes the Finnbot backup folders it uses in the owner's Drive.
- The backup data is stored only in the owner's Google Drive.
- It does not sell, share, or transfer Google user data to any third party.
- It does not use Google user data for advertising or to train machine learning models.
- It does not collect data from any other Google account.

## Retention and deletion

Backups stay in the owner's Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all further access.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this file.
